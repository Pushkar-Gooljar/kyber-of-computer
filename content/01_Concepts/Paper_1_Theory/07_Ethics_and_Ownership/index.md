## What to know about this topic

> [!danger] AI has no 9608 questions at all
> The 9608 Legend's AI section is **empty**. Every AI question in existence is 9618, 2021 onwards — and AI has appeared in **every series**. The `!info` and `!abstract` entries in 7.1.5 carry more weight than anywhere else in the vault.

> [!note] The smallest topic, but not the easiest
> 83 concepts is the lowest count in Paper 1, and the content is the least technical. That makes it tempting to skip — but the mark schemes are strict about **naming** things (the specific licence type, the specific ethical principle) rather than describing them loosely.

> [!info] Licensing types are where the marks are lost
> Free software, freeware, shareware and open source are **four distinct things** with four distinct permission sets. Questions ask for the differences, and approximate answers score nothing.
