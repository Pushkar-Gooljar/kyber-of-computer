## What to know about this topic

> [!danger] The only topic with **zero** 9608 entries
> 9618 rebuilt this topic so thoroughly that the old Legend contributes nothing usable — and **4.3 Bit manipulation has no 9608 questions whatsoever** (its Legend file is two pages with none). Bit manipulation is nonetheless examined in **every single series**. There is no old-syllabus safety net anywhere in this chapter.

> [!info] Cyclic shifts — named in the syllabus, examined zero times
> The notes name *"logical, arithmetic and cyclic"*. Logical shifts appear every series and arithmetic shifts twice recently; **cyclic shifts have never been examined**, and there is no 9608 fallback. The single clearest gap in the chapter.

> [!info] Every RTN question stops at the fetch
> The bullet is titled *stages of the **Fetch–Execute** cycle*, and the syllabus says "describe **and use** register transfer notation". Yet every 9618 question stops at `CIR ← [MDR]` — the fetch only. The decode and execute stages in RTN have never been asked.
