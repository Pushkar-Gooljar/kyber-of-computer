# Paper 1 — Theory Fundamentals

**75 marks · 1 hour 30 minutes · 50% of the AS Level**
No calculators. All questions compulsory, answered on the question paper.

All eight topics are written. Each has a **Jigsaw** (syllabus mapped to past questions) and a **Pack** (the same content as timed Q&A). If you have not met the system yet, read [[Jigsaw-Pack Architecture|the architecture note]] first.

---

## Topics

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/01_Information_Representation/index|1 · Information Representation]]
Number systems, base conversion, binary arithmetic, BCD, character sets, images, sound, compression. **168 concepts · 142 Q&A** — contains 1.1.3, the single most-asked bullet in the paper.

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/02_Communications/index|2 · Communications]]
Networks, the internet, protocols, transmission. **199 concepts · 157 Q&A** — the widest topic in the paper.

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/03_Hardware/index|3 · Hardware]]
Components, embedded systems, storage, buffers, RAM/ROM, sensors, logic gates. **94 concepts · 87 Q&A**

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/04_Processor_Fundamentals/index|4 · Processor Fundamentals]]
Von Neumann, registers, buses, the F–E cycle, interrupts, assembly, bit manipulation. **122 concepts · 114 Q&A**

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/05_System_Software/index|5 · System Software]]
Operating systems, utilities, program libraries, translators, IDEs. **109 concepts · 107 Q&A**

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/06_Security_Privacy_and_Data_Integrity/index|6 · Security, Privacy and Data Integrity]]
Threats, protection, encryption, digital signatures, validation and verification. **105 concepts · 110 Q&A**

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/07_Ethics_and_Ownership/index|7 · Ethics and Ownership]]
Professional ethics, copyright, licensing, artificial intelligence. **83 concepts · 82 Q&A**

### [[01_Concepts/Paper_1_Theory/08_Databases/index|8 · Databases]]
File-based limitations, relational models, E-R diagrams, normalisation, DBMS, SQL. **155 concepts · 148 Q&A** — the largest single topic.

## Reading the paper itself

> [!note] What the paper looks like
> Topics are examined roughly in syllabus order, one question per topic, with **Databases almost always a whole multi-part question** of 6–15 marks. Topic 4 reliably carries the assembly-language trace table, and topic 3 the logic circuit. The command word decides the tariff: *state* is 1 mark per point, *describe* is point-plus-expansion, *explain* needs a consequence, and *justify* usually puts **all** the marks in the reasoning rather than the choice.

> [!danger] Five sub-topics with no 9608 fallback
> These are new in 9618, so no old-syllabus questions exist to practise on:
> **AI** (7.1.5) · **embedded systems** (3.1.2) · **PROM/EPROM/EEPROM** (3.1.7) · **bit manipulation** (all of 4.3) · **IDE features** (5.2.4).
> All five are examined regularly. The `!info` inferences in those sections carry more weight than anywhere else in the vault.
