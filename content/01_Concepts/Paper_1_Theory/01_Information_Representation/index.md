## What to know about this topic

> [!note] The highest-frequency bullet in the whole paper
> **1.1.3 converting between bases** carries **51 separate 9618 questions** — more than any other single syllabus bullet in Paper 1. It is nearly guaranteed marks, and nearly guaranteed to appear.

> [!danger] The three slips that cost the most marks
> **1.** The unit divisor — 1000 for decimal prefixes (kB, MB), 1024 for binary ones (KiB, MiB). Read which the question used.
> **2.** Bit depth given in **bytes**, not bits — then there is no ÷8 (`9618_s24_qp_13_sc_2.a`).
> **3.** "Justify your choice" questions where the mark scheme awards **no mark for the choice itself**. All the marks are in the reasoning.

> [!warning] Three gaps where no question has ever been set
> **Hexadecimal applications** — bullet 1.1.5 is titled "BCD *and* Hexadecimal", yet every question in *both* series asks only about BCD. The clearest single gap in this topic.
> **Vector graphic compression** — named in bullet 1.3.3 alongside text, bitmap and sound; never asked in either series.
> **Quantisation error** — credited in six 9618 mark schemes, but no question ever asks you to define it.
> All three are written up as `!info` entries in the Jigsaw with the content you would need.
