## What to know about this topic

> [!note] The widest topic in the paper
> 199 mapped concepts — more than any other. It also has the **largest body of 9608 material still in syllabus** (35 entries), because networking changed least between the two syllabuses.

> [!warning] The "networking devices" trap
> The syllabus bullet *"benefits of networking devices"* means the benefits of **putting devices into a network** — not the benefits of network hardware like routers and switches. The 9608 Legend's author got this wrong and filed hardware questions under it; those questions are re-filed in the Jigsaw under the hardware bullets where they belong.
