## What to know about this topic

> [!danger] Two sub-topics are new in 9618
> **Embedded systems (3.1.2)** and **PROM/EPROM/EEPROM (3.1.7)** have **no 9608 questions at all**. Embedded systems has been examined in almost every series since 2021.

> [!info] The clearest untested gap: RAM vs ROM differences
> The bullet is titled *"explain the differences between RAM and ROM"*, yet **9618 has only ever asked what each one stores**. The differences question exists only in 9608. Volatility — the single most important fact — appears in 9618 only implicitly.

> [!note] 3.2 is a skills sub-topic, with fixed tariffs
> Draw a circuit from an expression is **always 2 marks**. Complete a truth table is **always 2**, marked in **blocks of four rows** — one wrong row loses half. Write an expression from a circuit is 2–3, marked per sub-expression. Practise the format, not the recall.
