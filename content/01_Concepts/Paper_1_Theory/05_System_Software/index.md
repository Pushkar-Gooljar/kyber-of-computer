## What to know about this topic

> [!danger] IDE features (5.2.4) is 9618-only — and the most examined bullet here
> The 9608 Legend has **no IDE questions at all**. 9618 has asked it **eleven times** since 2021, more than any other bullet in the chapter. Treat 5.2.4 as guaranteed, not an afterthought.

> [!warning] The second-largest body of 9608 material
> 32 entries — including an **8-mark sequencing question** on what happens when a file is read from the hard disk (`9608_w16_qp_12_sc_3`), which has no 9618 equivalent and is the longest single question in either series for this topic.

> [!note] The distractors in the utility questions
> A **translator**, an **IDE**, **graphics software** and a **spreadsheet** are **not** utility programs. That tick-table is 9608's, and the distractors are the whole question.
