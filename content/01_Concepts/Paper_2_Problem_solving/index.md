# Paper 2 — Fundamental Problem-solving and Programming

**75 marks · 2 hours · 50% of the AS Level**
Pseudocode follows the Cambridge *Pseudocode Guide for Teachers*.

> [!warning] Not yet built
> No Jigsaw or Pack notes exist for this paper. The sections below are the syllabus scope, so the structure is ready when the notes are written.

---

## Topics in scope

### 9 · Algorithm Design and Problem-solving
Abstraction and decomposition · algorithms as defined steps · structure charts, state-transition diagrams, pseudocode and flowcharts · stepwise refinement · identifying and correcting errors · writing and completing trace tables.

### 10 · Data Types and Structures
Data types and record structures · arrays (1D and 2D) · files (serial, sequential, random) · abstract data types — stacks, queues and linked lists.

### 11 · Programming
Programming basics · selection and iteration · built-in functions · structured programming with procedures, functions and parameters.

### 12 · Software Development
Program development lifecycle · testing strategies · test data selection · debugging and maintenance.

## Why this paper is different

> [!note] It is a skills paper, not a recall paper
> Paper 1 rewards knowing mark-scheme wording. Paper 2 rewards **writing working pseudocode under time pressure** and **tracing other people's code accurately**. The Jigsaw–Pack split still applies, but the Pack would be worked problems rather than recall questions. Practising past papers matters more here than anywhere else.
